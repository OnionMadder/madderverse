# The Madderverse Promise

*A binding statement of how we make games.*

*Internal document, May 2026. Not yet linked from public pages.*

---

## The promise

We make mobile games the way console games used to be.

The kind of game a parent could buy for their kid and know it had everything they needed. Self-contained. No internet required. No ads. No upsells. No surprises. Buy it once, own it forever.

When somebody plays a madderverse game, they get the whole game. Not a hook. Not a service. Not a trial that turns into a payment plan. The finished thing, in their hands, theirs.

---

## What this means in practice

- **No ads. Ever.** External ad networks, banners, video pre-rolls, native ads — none of them are allowed in any madderverse game.
- **No in-app purchases that gate play.** Players never pay for more time, more moves, more lives, more colors, or any other precondition for continuing to enjoy the game.
- **No subscriptions.** All purchases are one-time. Lifetime value compounds through ongoing free updates, not recurring charges.
- **Monetization fits the game.** Save-gate or premium one-time purchase for paid experiences. Freemium-with-cosmetic-packs for casual/kid-facing catalog titles. Never freemium-with-extraction.
- **Works offline.** Where a feature requires internet (community gallery, account sync), the absence of internet must NEVER break core gameplay.
- **Personal saves are sacred.** Nothing the player creates ever vanishes without their explicit understanding. Sharing creates a copy; the personal copy never moves.
- **Saves are celebrated.** Every save is treated as an achievement, not a draft, not a chore.
- **No timers that punish.** No streak-to-maintain. No "you'll lose your progress." Missing a day PAUSES, never RESETS.
- **No nagging notifications.** Push must add value, never manufacture FOMO.
- **Generosity over scarcity.** The free tier feels abundant. The Construction Paper Principle: a kid with a stack of fresh construction paper doesn't pay per sheet.
- **Mess is allowed.** Undo exists, never forced. There is no "wrong" output.
- **Each game serves its own audience first.** The catalog's umbrella voice is chaotic-cozy, but individual games audit their copy against their specific audience. The studio is not building for the dev — it is building for the people who will play.
- **The studio is human.** Marketing is honest. Reviews are answered. Bugs are fixed. The studio is not a corporation; the studio is a person making things.

## Fun friction — the only allowed substitute for predatory mechanics

Some genres rely on friction as part of their pacing. Match-3, idle, satisfying-cleanup, hole catcher, tile match — running out of moves, energy, or lives is part of the rhythm of those genres. Removing the friction would gut the genre.

The Madderverse Promise does not require sanitizing genres until they have no shape. It requires that every predatory EXIT from that friction be replaced with a fair one.

When a player hits a friction wall, the only exits permitted are:

- **A graceful time wait** — short (5-15 minutes), passive, optional, never the only path forward.
- **A minigame** — short, skill-based, that earns the player back what they lost.
- **A spoof ad** — a 3-second in-house comedic spot promoting another madderverse game, or absurdly promoting the game already being played.

Exits explicitly forbidden:

- **External ads** of any kind
- **Pay-to-continue** — the "spend $X to keep playing" mechanic
- **Long time-gates** — anything over 15 minutes that the player can pay to skip
- **Lootbox or random paid rewards** — slot-machine mechanics
- **Daily limits** — caps on how much a player can play

This is fun friction: the genre's rhythm preserved, the exploitation removed.

Spoof ads, when used, double as cross-promotional infrastructure for the catalog — every madderverse game becomes an entry point to every other madderverse game, in the studio's own voice, without paying external ad networks a cent.

## Privacy and trust

- **No tracking.** No third-party telemetry. Minimal aggregate analytics only (GoatCounter or equivalent).
- **Works offline.** Internet is a feature, never a requirement for core gameplay.
- **COPPA compliant** for kid-facing games.
- **Data minimization.** We collect only what's needed to make the game work. We delete on request, no questions.
- **All purchases are recoverable.** If you bought it, you own it forever, on any device, after any reinstall.

---

## On this promise

This promise is binding on every madderverse game from this point forward.

It can be amended when reality teaches us something new. When a principle proves wrong in practice, it changes. When something needs to be added, it gets added. This is a living document.

When two principles conflict in a specific decision, the version that treats the player more generously wins.
