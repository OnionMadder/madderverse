Gazonionaire drops you into an open galaxy with a ship, a credit balance, and a deadline. Your objective is simple: reach a target net worth before the clock runs out.

If this is your first run, the Tutorial is strongly recommended. It gives you double the time, a larger cargo hold, zero interest on loans, and a reduced event table — enough breathing room to learn the trade routes, understand how price trends work, and find your rhythm before the clock starts eating your margin.
