All Munkis

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-05-15 · Last updated: 2026-05-15

The short version

All Munkis collects nothing. No name, no email, no location, no contacts, no photos, no behavioral data, no advertising identifiers, no analytics of any kind.

All progress — which Munkis you've unlocked, your hidden achievements, your moon points — is stored only on your device. We never see it, never store it on a server, and never share it with anyone.

There are no ads. There are no in-app purchases. There is no login. There are no third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet at all — it works fully offline, forever.

Who makes All Munkis

All Munkis is made and published by Mad Sundar LLC (the “Studio,” “we,” or “us”), operating under Madderverse umbrella at madderverse.org.

You can reach a human at support@madderverse.org.

What information we collect

None. Specifically, All Munkis does not request, capture, transmit, or otherwise collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (names, email, phone, mailing address)
  • Account identifiers, sign-in credentials, or social profiles
  • Photos, camera input, microphone input, or any media on the device
  • Precise location, IP-based location, or any geolocation data
  • Contacts, calendar entries, health data, or device sensors
  • Advertising identifiers (AAID), device fingerprints, or analytics events
  • Behavioral data, usage telemetry, error reports, or crash logs

The app version of All Munkis makes zero network requests. It has no analytics beacon, loads no web fonts, and contacts no servers — not ours, not anyone's. Everything it needs is bundled inside the app.

What the app stores on your device

All Munkis saves your game progress — unlocked Munkis, the hidden achievements you've discovered, your moon-point total, and which “seventh wheel” Munki you last chose — to the device's local storage, inside the app's private sandbox. On Android we also mirror this to the operating system's app-preferences store so it survives a WebView data clear. It never leaves the device.

Uninstalling All Munkis deletes this progress. There is no cloud backup and no account, by design — which is also why there is nothing for anyone to breach, sell, or subpoena.

COPPA & GDPR-K

All Munkis is designed for children, including those under 13 (US) and under 16 (EU). Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to disclose to parents, no profile to build, and no consent to manage:

  • COPPA (US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act): we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 — or from anyone.
  • GDPR-K (EU): no personal data is processed.
  • Google Play Families Policy / Designed for Families: no ads, no in-app purchases, no third-party analytics, no unfiltered external links, no data collection.

Links out of the app

The app build of All Munkis has no links that leave the app — the cross-site navigation present on the web version at madderverse.org is removed from the packaged app specifically so a child can't tap out to the open web.

Third-party services

None. There are no third-party SDKs, analytics, ad networks, or fonts loaded from anyone. The only software bundled in the app besides our own is the open-source Tone.js audio library (MIT licensed), which runs entirely on-device and makes no network calls.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add data collection, advertising, or third-party services to All Munkis (we don't plan to), we will update this page with a new effective date before the change ships, and document exactly what is collected and how to opt out. The dates at the top of this page reflect the last meaningful revision.
