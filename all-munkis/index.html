THE SYNTH-SQUAD CHRONICLES

The Synth-Squad keeps the beat humming through the neon. Srivi-Bot, Green Gear, Amber Arp, and their friends each carry a sound that fits the loop. They glow in every color: blue, green, orange, purple, red, yellow.

Every crew has a BLACK Watcher who anchors the rhythm and keeps the others safe. Shadow Pulse stands sentinel for the daytime crew. Mega Thump grounds the night crew. Two more Watchers wait somewhere deeper in the system — no one's met them yet.

And then there are the WHITE Munkis. Four of them, one for each crew. Ice Munki flash-freezes the rhythm into sharp chimes. Moon Munki pulls every note into a silent void. Void Munki and Static Munki are still rumors — said to delete the music and tear the speakers — but no one knows where they hide.

When a White meets the loop, the lights flicker, the code glitches, and the room goes WRONG. That is HORROR MODE.

In the distorted shadows of the system, where the data is corrupted and the music sounds different, lives a crew of outcasts known as THE MADBALLZ.

The Whites are their favorite guests. They don't want to chill; they want to break the speakers and rewire the world. They share secrets, they share static, and they are always looking for new friends.

If you've been WRONG enough times… you might just find the key to their lair.
