DELETE MY POOTERY DATA

Right to be forgotten — your data, your call.

FASTEST WAY: IN-APP

If you can still sign in to Pootery on a phone, the quickest path is the in-app button:

  1. Open Pootery on your phone
  2. Tap the menu → ACCOUNT
  3. Scroll to DATA & PRIVACY
  4. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT

It removes your profile, every pot you shared, every battle entry, and every vote — server-side and on your device — in one tap. Takes about 5 seconds.

CAN'T SIGN IN? EMAIL US

Lost access to your phone or your sign-in? Email the address below from the email you used to register for Pootery. We'll confirm it's you and delete everything within 30 days, usually within a few.

pootery@madderverse.org

What to include:

  • The email address tied to your account (so we can match it).
  • Your Pootery handle (e.g., @yourname), if you remember it.
  • Anything else that helps us confirm it's you (a public pot's name, a battle you started, etc.) — optional but helpful for accounts where the email alone is ambiguous.

WHAT GETS DELETED

Hard delete — not hidden, not anonymized. Includes:

  • Your profile (handle, display name, bio)
  • Every pot you shared to the EVERYONE gallery
  • Every battle you started
  • Every pot you entered in battles
  • Every vote you cast
  • Your sign-in identity (Google / magic-link record)

Other users' votes on your pots vanish along with the pots themselves (cascade). Other users' battles you voted in keep their other entries; only your votes are removed.

Anything saved only on your device (gallery, drafts, achievements) requires the in-app WIPE THIS DEVICE button, since we can't reach into your phone from here. Email-based deletion handles server-side only.

QUESTIONS?

Same email — pootery@madderverse.org. Real human reads it. We answer in plain English.
