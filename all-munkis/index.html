THE RAINBOW & THE LEFT-OUT MUNKIS

Six Munkis make the rainbow. Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, and Purple. Together they shine, and together they keep the loop going.

And then there are the two who don't fit. Ice Munki is too cold to be a color. Moon Munki is too dark. When the rainbow gets together, those two have to watch from the side.

They wish you'd pick them. You'll hear them say so if you stop by.

If one of them DOES land on the stage — well. The room goes a little WRONG. The lights flicker. The music shivers. That's HORROR MODE. Ice freezes the others. Moon makes the whole room weird.

It's not because they're mean. It's because they're not used to being chosen.

Moon is shy. He's hiding somewhere on this page. Try things. Tap things. Maybe he'll show up.
