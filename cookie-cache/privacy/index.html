Privacy Policy

Tub's Cookie Cache · Last updated July 3, 2026

Tub's Cookie Cache is built to be safe and self-contained. It's an ad-free game for kids, and it collects nothing about you or your child.

What the game stores

The game keeps your high score and your chosen difficulty (Cozy or Classic) stored locally on your device, so the game can remember your best score and settings between sessions. That's it — a couple of numbers. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or made available to us or anyone else. We have no access to them.

The game requires no account and no sign-in, and it works fully offline.

Information we collect

The Tub's Cookie Cache app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking — no advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind. It makes no calls to any server about you.

Purchases

If you bought Tub's Cookie Cache on Google Play, the purchase is handled entirely by Google. We do not receive or store your name, email, or payment details. Please see Google Play's own privacy policy for how it handles purchases.

Children

Tub's Cookie Cache is made for kids and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game collects no personal information at all, it is safe for all ages.

Data sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we don't collect any. There is nothing to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at support@madsundar.com.
