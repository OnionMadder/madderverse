THE RAINBOW & THE CURSED MUNKIS

Six Munkis make the rainbow. Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, and Purple. Together they shine, and together they keep the loop going.

And then there are the two who don't fit. Ice Munki is too cold to be a color. Moon Munki is too dark. When the rainbow gets together, those two have to watch from the side. They wish you'd pick them — you'll hear them say so if you stop by.

It's not because they're mean. It's because they're not used to being chosen. But jealousy that old turns sharp. Long ago, Ice and Moon reached out and cursed some of the rainbow Munkis — twisting them, giving them wings, and casting them out.

Those are the Flying Creeps: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, and Purple, each warped into a winged, sorrowful thing. Forever they drift back to the stage — not to rejoin the rainbow, but to ruin the performance they can never be part of again.

And if Ice or Moon themselves land on the stage — the room goes a little WRONG. The lights flicker. The music shivers. That's HORROR MODE.

Moon is shy. He's hiding somewhere on this page. Try things. Tap things. Maybe he'll show up.
