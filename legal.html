Privacy

We've designed the Site to collect as little information as possible. We do not run ads, we do not sell data, and we do not build user profiles.

How this works across games. Most games on The Madderverse are entirely self-contained — they run in your browser, have no accounts, and send us nothing beyond the aggregate analytics described below. A few games offer optional online features such as a sign-in or a shared gallery. Those games are clearly marked and each has its own privacy & legal page that explains exactly what it collects and how to delete it; for that game, its own page controls. This currently applies to: Let's CRAYte! Pootery — see the Pootery Privacy & Legal page.

What we collect

  • Aggregate visit counts via GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. GoatCounter records the page you visited, your browser type, screen size, the referring page, and a country derived from your IP address. No cookies are set, no personal information is stored, and no profiles are built. GoatCounter does not share data with advertisers.
  • Local game progress — high scores, saved pots, achievements, and similar in-game state — is stored on your device using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • Optional public submissions in Let's CRAYte! Pootery. When you choose to submit a pot to the public gallery, enter a head-to-head pot battle, or vote on a battle entry, the following is sent to a third-party database (Supabase, described under “Third parties” below) so other players can see it:
    • The pot you made (its shape data and the painted glaze image).
    • The name you give the pot (up to 40 characters; optional).
    • The byline you choose (up to 40 characters; optional, defaults to “anonymous”).
    • A random per-browser identifier (UUID) generated the first time you submit or vote. It is used only to prevent double-voting and to mark entries as “yours” on this device. It is not tied to any real-world identity. Clearing your browser data resets it.
    Anyone using the public gallery or battles features can see your submitted pot, its name, and its byline. Please do not enter your real name, email address, phone number, school, location, or any other personal information into the name or byline fields. If you regret a submission, contact us via madsundar.com and we will remove it.

What we do not collect

  • Email addresses, phone numbers, real names, or other directly identifying information, in the core self-contained games. (Games with an optional account — currently Let's CRAYte! Pootery — may collect an email or Google sign-in for that account; see that game's privacy page.)
  • Account credentials for the core self-contained games (those games have no accounts or sign-ins).
  • Precise location data; only country-level visit aggregates are recorded by GoatCounter.
  • Tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, browser fingerprints, or social-media trackers.
  • Microphone, camera, contacts, or device sensor data.
  • Any information from children directly. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from any visitor, including children.

User-submitted content in Let's CRAYte! Pootery

The public gallery and pot battles in Let's CRAYte! Pootery let players share their creations with each other. Submissions are visible to all other players. Please keep submissions appropriate for a general audience: no sexually explicit imagery, no hate symbols, no threats or harassment, no personal information about yourself or others, and nothing else that violates these terms. Every gallery and battle entry has a Report button you can use to flag inappropriate content. We review reports and remove offending submissions; serious or repeated violations may result in the underlying device identifier being blocked from future submissions. Reports can also be filed directly through madsundar.com.

Games that offer an optional account or online features — currently only Let's CRAYte! Pootery — are intended for ages 13 and up and are not directed to children under 13; what they collect and how to delete it is described on that game's own privacy page.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided personal information to us, please contact us via madsundar.com and we will promptly investigate and, if applicable, delete the information.

Third parties

The Site uses two third-party services:

  • GoatCounter for aggregate visit analytics, described above. Used site-wide.
  • Supabase hosts the database that stores public pot submissions, battle entries, and votes for Let's CRAYte! Pootery. Supabase processes only the data described under “What we collect” above — submitted pots, optional names and bylines, and a random per-browser identifier — and only when you choose to use the public features. Their handling of that data is governed by Supabase's privacy policy.

We do not embed advertising networks, social-media trackers, chat widgets, or other third-party scripts. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages; standard server logs maintained by GitHub may apply to the connection itself, governed by GitHub's privacy statement.
