Privacy Policy

Tiny Canvas · Last updated August 5, 2026

Tiny Canvas is built to be safe and self-contained. It's an ad-free coloring app for kids, and it collects nothing about you or your child.

What the app stores

Tiny Canvas keeps your child's drawings, the drawing currently in progress, and their settings (brush smoothing, sound, music, language) stored locally on the device, so work isn't lost between sessions. On phones and tablets these are also mirrored into the app's own private storage so they survive an app restart.

That's all of it. Those items are never uploaded, transmitted, or made available to us or to anyone else. We have no access to them, and no way to see what your child draws.

The app requires no account and no sign-in, and it works fully offline.

Information we collect

Tiny Canvas does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking — no advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind. It makes no calls to any server about you.

Unlike the rest of the Madderverse website, this app deliberately loads no analytics beacon at all, and its fonts are bundled inside the app rather than fetched from a font service — so simply opening it does not tell any third party that you did.

Saving and sharing drawings

Exporting a drawing hands the image to your device's own share or download function, which is operated by your phone, tablet or browser — not by us. Where the picture goes from there is entirely your choice. Tiny Canvas never uploads a drawing anywhere on its own.

Children

Tiny Canvas is made for kids and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it is safe for all ages.

Actions that would leave the app — exporting a picture, deleting saved work, or opening an external link — sit behind a short grown-up check, so a young child cannot trigger them alone.

Purchases

If you obtained Tiny Canvas through an app store, that transaction is handled entirely by the store. We do not receive or store your name, email, or payment details. Please see that store's own privacy policy for how it handles purchases. Tiny Canvas contains no in-app purchases.

Data sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we don't collect any. There is nothing to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at support@madsundar.com.
