Privacy Policy

Slip Studio · Last updated May 27, 2026

Slip Studio is built to be quiet and self-contained. Everything you make stays on your device, and the app collects nothing about you.

What the app stores

The pots you shape, glaze, decorate, and save are stored locally on your device (in your device's app storage). They are never uploaded, transmitted, or made available to us or anyone else. We have no access to them.

The app requires no account and no sign-in, and it works fully offline.

Information we collect

The Slip Studio app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking — no advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind. It uses no cookies and makes no calls to any server about you.

Purchases

If you bought Slip Studio on Google Play, the purchase is handled entirely by Google. We do not receive or store your name, email, or payment details. Please see Google Play's own privacy policy for how it handles purchases.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it is safe for all ages.

Data sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we don't collect any. There is nothing to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at support@madsundar.com.
