Privacy Policy

Groodle · Last updated August 19, 2026

Groodle is built to be safe and self-contained. It's a free, ad-free drawing toy for kids, and it collects nothing about you or your child.

Your child's drawings stay on the device

When your child taps Save, the drawing is written to storage on that device only. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or made available to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it. The Gallery inside the app shows only the drawings saved on that same device, and any of them can be deleted from the app at any time.

There is no shared or public gallery, no way to send a drawing to another person from inside the app, and no way for your child to see anyone else's drawings.

What else the app stores

Alongside the drawings, Groodle remembers a few small values locally on the device so it can pick up where your child left off:

  • Doodles earned, and which hats have been unlocked or are being worn
  • Achievements unlocked
  • Preferences such as the chosen pose, background, color and brush size

That's it. These never leave the device, and we have no access to them. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Information we collect

The Groodle app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking — no advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind.

The app makes no network requests at all. It requires no account and no sign-in, asks for no permissions beyond what Android needs to run it, and works fully offline.

Purchases

Groodle is completely free. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no paid content. The app contains no billing code and no payment SDK of any kind.

Children

Groodle is made for kids and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it is safe for all ages.

Data sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we don't collect any. There is nothing to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
