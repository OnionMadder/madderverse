Copy-paste source for the **Play Console Main store listing**. Voice = playful,
punchy, kid-and-parent friendly — matches the game's arcade tone. Audience =
**Everyone / Designed for Families** (no social, no UGC, no network calls after
install). Pricing = **paid app, one-time price, no in-app purchases, no ads**.

> The web build at https://madderverse.org/cookie-cache/ is the de-facto trial —
> the store listing points to it as a free browser preview so buyers can try the
> game before paying. There is intentionally no Play-side trial, no IAP, and no
> ads. (See CLAUDE.md → "Slip Studio Android paid app build" for the shared

## Full description  (max 4000 chars)

```
Meet Tub Butter — a fuzzy orange data-eater who lives inside your computer. Tub's job is to keep his Cache tidy by gobbling up cookies as they fly in. Your job is to help him.

Cookies come flying across the screen from both sides. Tap them as fast as you can before they sail off the edge! But watch out — Tub is PICKY. Random veggies drift in too, and if you tap one, he wails "YUCK!" and your streak crashes. Grab the cookies, dodge the greens, and build the biggest streak you can before the timer runs out.

Then comes the best part: the FEAST. Every cookie you caught piles up, and Tub chomps the whole stack in a happy, crunchy, burp-at-the-end finale.

A QUICK, HONEST ARCADE GAME
• Fast 30-second rounds — perfect for a quick play or a "just one more"
• Tap-to-catch controls any kid can pick up in seconds
• Build a streak for x2, x3, all the way to x5 INSANE combos
• Beat your own high score — it's saved right on your device
• A satisfying feast finale after every round

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
• COZY — slower cookies, a gentle pace, and no penalties. Made for the youngest players (ages 4–5).
• CLASSIC — the full arcade rush, ramping faster and faster to a frantic finish.

IT SECRETLY TEACHES SOMETHING REAL
Tap Tub on the start screen to open a little storybook. In four short pages it explains — in words a kid actually gets — what a "cookie" really is on the real internet: the little notes websites leave to remember you, which ones are friendly, and which ones are the sneaky kind grown-ups argue about. It's the clearest explanation of web cookies your kid will ever get, hidden inside a game they'll want to replay.

SAFE BY DESIGN
• No ads. None.
• No in-app purchases. You pay once and you're done.
• No accounts, no sign-in, no data collection.
• Works completely offline.
• Made by a parent, for kids.

Tub's Cookie Cache is part of the Madderverse — a small collection of safe, ad-free games for curious kids, made by Mad Sundar LLC. Try this one free in your browser first at madderverse.org/cookie-cache.

Tap START. Tub is HUNGRY.
```

## Store settings checklist  (user does these in Play Console)

- **App name:** `Tub's Cookie Cache`
- **Package / Application ID:** `org.madderverse.cookiecache`
- **Category:** Games → Arcade  (or Casual)
- **Pricing:** Paid, one-time. Match the Madderverse price point (Slip Studio = $2.99).
- **In-app purchases:** None
- **Ads:** Contains ads = **No**
- **Content rating:** complete the IARC questionnaire → expected **Everyone**
- **Target audience:** include children — enroll in **Designed for Families / Teacher Approved** track (no ads + no data collection makes this clean)
- **Data safety form:** "No data collected, no data shared" (localStorage high-score never leaves the device)
- **Privacy policy URL:** `https://madderverse.org/cookie-cache/privacy/`
