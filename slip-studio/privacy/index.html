Privacy Policy

Slip Studio · Last updated October 6, 2026

Slip Studio is built to be quiet and self-contained. Everything you make stays on your device. The only information that ever leaves it is the record of an optional purchase, used to unlock what you paid for.

What the app stores

The pots you shape, glaze, decorate, and save are stored locally on your device (in your device's app storage). They are never uploaded, transmitted, or made available to us or anyone else. We have no access to them.

The app requires no account and no sign-in, and it works fully offline.

Information we collect

Slip Studio does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or anything you make. It contains no ads, no usage analytics, and no tracking — no advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind, and no advertising ID. It uses no cookies.

The one exception is purchase history, described below. It exists only if a grown-up chooses to buy the optional Pro unlock (or bought the app before it became free).

Purchases

Slip Studio is free to download. A one-time Slip Studio Pro unlock is available behind a parent gate. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your name, email, or payment details.

To confirm a purchase and unlock Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-validation service. When the app starts, it sends RevenueCat an anonymous, randomly generated app identifier and Google Play's purchase receipt, so that Pro can be unlocked and restored. RevenueCat keeps the purchase history (what was bought, and when) linked to that anonymous identifier, which we can see in RevenueCat's dashboard. It is not linked to your name or account, and it is never used for advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

If you bought Slip Studio before it became free, the app checks the date it was installed on your device to unlock Pro for you. That date is never sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Your pots live only on your device: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them. To have the purchase history for your device deleted, email support@madsundar.com with the subject "Slip Studio data deletion". Tell us roughly when the purchase was made; we'll delete the matching RevenueCat record within 30 days and confirm by reply. Google Play keeps its own record of the payment under its own policy.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases sit behind a parent gate, and the purchase record contains no personal information.

Data sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent, or share data with anyone. RevenueCat processes purchase records on our behalf only to validate purchases.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at support@madsundar.com.
