Tiny Canvas

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-05-14 · Last updated: 2026-05-14

The short version

Tiny Canvas does not collect any personal information from children or adults. No name, no email, no location, no contacts, no photos from the camera roll, no behavioral data, no advertising identifiers.

Drawings your child makes are stored only on the device, inside the app's sandbox. We never see them, store them on a server, or share them with anyone.

There are no ads. There are no in-app purchases. There are no third-party SDKs that track your child or report back to us.

Who runs Tiny Canvas

Tiny Canvas is made and published by Mad Sundar LLC (the "Studio," "we," or "us"). The studio operates under the Madderverse umbrella at madderverse.org.

You can reach a human at support@madderverse.org.

What information we collect

None.

Tiny Canvas does not request, capture, transmit, or otherwise collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (names, email, phone, mailing address)
  • Account identifiers, sign-in credentials, or social profiles
  • Photos from the camera roll, camera input, microphone input, or any media stored on the device outside the app
  • Precise location, IP-based location, or any geolocation data
  • Contacts, calendar entries, health data, or device sensors
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, AAID), device fingerprints, or analytics events
  • Behavioral data, usage telemetry, error reports, or crash logs

The drawings your child creates are saved to the device's local storage (or the app's private storage on iOS / Android). We do not have access to them.

COPPA & GDPR-K

Tiny Canvas is designed for children, including those under 13 (US) and under 16 (EU). Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we have no information to share with third parties, no profile to build, and no consent to manage. That puts Tiny Canvas comfortably inside the rules:

  • COPPA (US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act): We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We have nothing to disclose to parents because there is nothing collected.
  • GDPR-K (EU General Data Protection Regulation for children): No personal data is processed.
  • Apple App Store Kids Category: Tiny Canvas contains no third-party analytics, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no unfiltered external links. Links that leave the app sit behind a parental gate.
  • Google Play Designed for Families: Same.

What sits behind the parent gate

Some actions in Tiny Canvas open something outside the kid's drawing flow. Each of those is behind a math-problem gate that an adult solves:

  • Exporting a drawing to the device (Save / Share)
  • Deleting a saved drawing from the gallery
  • Tapping the home button that links back to madderverse.org
  • Tapping links inside the app footer

The gate is a two-digit addition problem. Once a parent solves it once during a session, those actions remain unlocked until the app closes.

Data your child creates

Drawings, brush settings, and the "first drawing" celebration flag are stored locally on the device. On iOS and Android we mirror this state to the operating system's preferences store so it survives webview clears, but it never leaves the device.

Deleting Tiny Canvas removes the drawings. If your child has work they want to keep, use the EXPORT button inside a saved drawing (behind the parent gate) to save it to the device's photo library or files app first.

Third-party services

None. There are no third-party SDKs embedded in Tiny Canvas.

Tiny Canvas does load web fonts from Google Fonts (Bungee, VT323, Press Start 2P). Google Fonts may receive your device's IP address as part of that font request. We do not send Google Fonts any other information.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add data collection, advertising, or third-party services to Tiny Canvas (we don't plan to), we will update this page with an effective date before the change ships, and we will document what is collected and how to opt out.

"Effective" + "Last updated" dates at the top of this page tell you when this was last meaningfully revised.
