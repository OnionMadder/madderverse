The Story of All Munkis

Once upon a beat, fourteen friends made a song.

Reginald Cotswattle. Srivatsan Sivanesan. JerryBerry Jamband. Twinkle Tessellate. Hubert Hubcap. Plopplop Tropicalo. The whole crew. They wore funny hats. They had giant headphones. They were great.

Then Ice Munki showed up. She freezes the song.

Then Moon Munki showed up. He dims the lights.

When they step onto the stage, the room goes WRONG. That is HORROR MODE.

Across town, in a place where the music sounds different, there lives a crew called THE MADBALLZ.

Ice Munki and Moon Munki visit them all the time. They share snacks. They share secrets. They are friends.

If you've been WRONG enough times… you can meet them too.
