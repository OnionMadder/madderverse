Privacy Policy

Tiny Canvas · Last updated October 7, 2026

Tiny Canvas is built to be safe and self-contained. It's an ad-free coloring app for kids. Nothing about you or your child is collected, and drawings never leave the device. The only information that ever leaves it is the record of an optional grown-up purchase, used to unlock what was paid for.

What the app stores

Tiny Canvas keeps your child's drawings, the drawing currently in progress, and their settings (brush smoothing, sound, music, language) stored locally on the device, so work isn't lost between sessions. On phones and tablets these are also mirrored into the app's own private storage so they survive an app restart.

That's all of it. Those items are never uploaded, transmitted, or made available to us or to anyone else. We have no access to them, and no way to see what your child draws.

The app requires no account and no sign-in, and it works fully offline.

Information we collect

Tiny Canvas does not collect your or your child's name, email, location, contacts, photos, or drawings. It contains no ads, no usage analytics, and no tracking — no advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind, and no advertising ID.

The one exception is purchase history for the optional Pro upgrade, described under "Purchases" below.

Unlike the rest of the Madderverse website, this app deliberately loads no analytics beacon at all, and its fonts are bundled inside the app rather than fetched from a font service — so simply opening it does not tell any analytics or font service that you did. (On phones and tablets the app does check in with RevenueCat at start-up, only to know whether Pro is unlocked — see "Purchases".)

Saving and sharing drawings

Exporting a drawing hands the image to your device's own share or download function, which is operated by your phone, tablet or browser — not by us. Where the picture goes from there is entirely your choice. Tiny Canvas never uploads a drawing anywhere on its own.

Children

Tiny Canvas is made for kids and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only record that ever leaves the device is a purchase record, which contains no personal information.

Actions that would leave the app — exporting a picture, deleting saved work, or opening an external link — sit behind a short grown-up check, so a young child cannot trigger them alone.

Purchases

If you obtained Tiny Canvas through an app store, that transaction is handled entirely by the store. We do not receive or store your name, email, or payment details. Please see that store's own privacy policy for how it handles purchases.

The app-store versions offer one optional, one-time in-app purchase: the Tiny Canvas Pro upgrade (extra brushes, stamps, papers, and frames — not a subscription; everything the free app does stays free). Payment is handled by the store. To confirm the purchase and restore it after a reinstall, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-validation service. When the app starts on a phone or tablet, it sends RevenueCat a random, anonymous per-install identifier and the store's purchase receipt, so Pro can be unlocked and restored. RevenueCat keeps the purchase history (what was bought, and when) linked to that anonymous identifier, which we can see in RevenueCat's dashboard. It contains nothing about your child, no drawings, and nothing that identifies you personally, and it is never used for advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. The purchase and "Restore Purchases" buttons sit behind the parental gate. The web version at madderverse.org has no purchases and never contacts RevenueCat.

Deleting your data

Drawings and settings live only on the device: deleting them in the app, uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage removes them. To have the purchase history for your device deleted, email support@madsundar.com with the subject "Tiny Canvas data deletion". Tell us roughly when the purchase was made; we'll delete the matching RevenueCat record within 30 days and confirm by reply. The app store keeps its own record of the payment under its own policy.

Data sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent, or share data with anyone. RevenueCat processes purchase records on our behalf only to validate purchases.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at support@madsundar.com.
